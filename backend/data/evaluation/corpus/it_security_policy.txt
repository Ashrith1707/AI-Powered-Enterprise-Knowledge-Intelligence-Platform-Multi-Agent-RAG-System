Information Security Policy
Document ID: POL-7701 | Version 2.4 | Department: IT Security | Category: Policy
Approved by: Chief Information Security Officer

1. Password Standard
All corporate account passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not contain the
user name. Passwords expire every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for
VPN, email, and all administrative consoles. Password sharing is prohibited and is treated as
a disciplinary offence.

2. Device Security
Full-disk encryption is required on all laptops and mobile devices. Screen lock activates after
10 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may access email only through the managed container.

3. Security Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident must be reported to the security operations centre within 24
hours using the incident portal or the hotline. Critical incidents involving customer data must
be escalated to the CISO within 4 hours. Incident reports are reviewed weekly.

4. Phishing Awareness
All employees complete phishing awareness training every quarter. Users who fail two simulated
phishing tests must repeat the training module.

5. Access Control
Access to production systems follows the principle of least privilege. Access reviews are
performed quarterly by system owners.
