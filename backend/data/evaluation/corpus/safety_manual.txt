Industrial Machine Safety Manual
Document ID: SOP-4021 | Version 3.2 | Department: Operations | Category: Manual
Prepared by: Plant Safety Committee

1. Lockout-Tagout Procedure
All maintenance personnel must perform lockout-tagout (LOTO) before servicing any machine.
The authorized technician must isolate all energy sources, apply a personal padlock, and attach
a tag showing the technician name and date. Maintenance work on energized equipment is strictly
prohibited. Group lockout requires a secondary verification by the shift supervisor.

2. Personal Protective Equipment
Safety glasses, steel-toe boots, and cut-resistant gloves are mandatory on the production floor.
Hearing protection is required in zones where noise exceeds 85 decibels. Welding operations
require a full face shield and flame-resistant clothing.

3. Authorized Maintenance Personnel
Only technicians who completed the Level-2 Maintenance Certification may open machine guards.
Contractors must be escorted by a certified employee and registered at the front desk. The
maintenance supervisor approves all non-routine repairs.

4. Emergency Stop
Emergency stop buttons are located at every workstation and must be tested weekly. After an
emergency stop event, the machine may only be restarted after a supervisor inspection.

5. Inspection Schedule
Machines undergo a documented safety inspection every 90 days. Inspection records are retained
for five years and audited annually by the safety committee.
