Employee Leave Policy
Document ID: POL-1102 | Version 5.0 | Department: Human Resources | Category: Policy
Effective date: 2024-01-15 | Author: HR Operations Team

1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave
accrues at 2 days per month starting from the date of joining. Employees may carry over a
maximum of 5 unused leave days to the following year; any additional balance lapses on
31 December.

2. Sick Leave
Each employee receives 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave beyond two consecutive
days requires a medical certificate. Unused sick leave is not carried over.

3. Leave Application Process
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance.
The reporting manager approves or rejects requests within 2 working days. Leave during the
peak audit season (March) requires director approval.

4. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks as per statutory requirements. Paternity leave is 10 working days.

5. Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave of up to 30 days per year may be granted in exceptional circumstances with HR
director approval.
