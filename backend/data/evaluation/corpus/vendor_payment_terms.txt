Vendor Contract and Payment Guide
Document ID: FIN-3300 | Version 1.8 | Department: Finance | Category: Contract
Owner: Accounts Payable

1. Standard Payment Terms
The standard payment terms for all vendors are net-30 days from receipt of a valid invoice.
Strategic vendors with an annual spend above one million dollars may negotiate net-45 terms
with CFO approval. Late payment disputes must be raised within 10 days of the due date.

2. Vendor Onboarding
New vendors must submit tax registration, bank details, and a signed code of conduct before
the first purchase order is issued. The procurement team completes vendor due diligence
within 15 working days.

3. Purchase Orders
A purchase order is mandatory for any spend above 500 dollars. Invoices without a matching
purchase order are rejected automatically by the accounts payable system.

4. Invoice Requirements
Invoices must quote the purchase order number, vendor tax ID, and bank reference. Electronic
invoicing through the vendor portal is preferred over email submissions.
